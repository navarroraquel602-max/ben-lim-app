Privacy Policy

Glory App by Ben Lim Global · Effective date: to be set by the ministry

The Glory App is provided by Ben Lim Global, LLC ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

What we do not do

  • The app has no user accounts and no sign-in.
  • The app has no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking tools.
  • We do not sell your information.

Information you choose to send us

Mission and crusade application. If you apply for a mission or crusade, the app sends your answers to our missions team at missions@benlimglobal.com. The application asks for:

  • contact details: name, email, phone number and address
  • date of birth and gender
  • church background and ministry affiliation
  • health information: known allergies, dietary restrictions, and any medical or emotional conditions or treatment
  • your agreement to the applicant, disciplinary and media releases, and your typed signature

We use this only to review your application and to plan the trip safely. To deliver it, the app passes your answers through FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), an email-forwarding service, which sends them to our missions inbox. If that service cannot be reached, your phone's email app opens with your answers filled in, and nothing is sent until you press send.

Other requests. Booking requests, oil and prayer shawl orders, call requests, and hub notes open your phone's email app with a message addressed to hello@benlimglobal.com. Nothing is sent unless you send that email yourself.

Information kept only on your phone

The app saves a few things on your device so it works the way you left it. This information stays on your phone and is not sent to us:

  • the language you chose
  • your progress in the "21 Days in John" reading plan
  • your Prophecy Now "Amen" day count
  • copies of missions, events, and prophecy library content, so the app can show them without an internet connection

Deleting the app, or clearing its website data in your browser, removes this information.

Content loaded from other services

  • benlimglobal.com. Missions, events and the prophecy library are loaded from our website, which receives the normal technical details of each request (such as your IP address).
  • Google Fonts. The app's fonts are loaded from Google, which also receives your IP address when it delivers them.

Links to other services

Some buttons open other services, each with its own privacy policy. These include Tithe.ly (giving), Square (checkout), Teachable (courses), Amazon, Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, Linktree, and benlimfuego.com. Payments are handled entirely by those services; the app never sees your card details.

How long we keep information

Applications and emails you send are kept in our ministry inboxes for as long as needed to respond and plan our missions, and then deleted. [The ministry should confirm this period.]

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has sent us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct, or delete the information you sent us by emailing hello@benlimglobal.com.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes how it handles information (for example, when partner sign-in is added), we will update this page and change the effective date.
